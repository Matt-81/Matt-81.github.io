### Short bio (Mon 17 Aug 08:57)

I hold a **Master's Degree in *Philosophy*** and a **Ph.D. in *Computer Science***, and I have the **Italian National Scientific Habilitation *(Abilitazione Scientifica Nazionale - ASN)*** for **Logic and Philosopy of Science**. My research spans the fields of **Conceptual Modeling**, **Knowledge Discovery**, and **Knowledge Representation**, with a particular focus on advancing the *theoretical foundations* of information systems design and developing *semi-automated solutions* for the creation of conceptual models. Other key research interests include **Logic** and **Philosophy of Language**. 

I am currently a **Senior Lecturer** at the [Department of Computer and Systems Sciences](https://www.su.se/english/divisions/department-of-computer-and-systems-sciences), [University of Stockholm](https://www.su.se/).

I was a member of the *KRDB group* at the [Free University of Bozen-Bolzano](https://www.unibz.it/), where I lectured in *Information Systems Design* and *Research Methods*. I also collaborate with the Services and Cybersecurity Group at the [University of Twente](https://www.utwente.nl/), and with the [Institute of Cognitive Sciences and Technologies (ISTC-CNR)](https://www.istc.cnr.it/). Additionally, I collaborate with the Knowdive Group at the Department of Information Engineering and Computer Science of the [University of Trento](https://www.unitn.it/), where I obtained my Ph.D. and, from 2015 to 2020, I served as a *Teaching Assistant* for *Computational Logics* and *Knowledge Graphs and Data Integration*.
